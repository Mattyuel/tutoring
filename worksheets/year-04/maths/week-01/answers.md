## Teacher notes
- **Common misconceptions:** forgetting placeholder zeros (writing *ninety thousand and ninety* as 9 090); reading the digit instead of its value; rounding by looking at the wrong digit; "rippling" when rounding numbers like 71 995.
- **If the student finishes early:** the Fast-finisher bank runs ~35 min; F7 and F10 are open-ended enough to fill any remaining time (e.g. "Write 99 999 in Egyptian symbols").
- **If the student struggles:** use a physical place-value chart and counters (F7 is a good hands-on activity); MAB blocks for renaming.
- **Sources / fact-check:** Egyptian hieroglyphic numerals — stroke (1), hobble/heel bone (10), coil of rope (100), lotus (1 000), finger (10 000), tadpole/frog (100 000), the god Heh (1 000 000). Crowd, towns and odometer figures are invented for the problems.
- **Next week builds on:** rounding → estimating and checking addition/subtraction; renaming → regrouping in written algorithms.
