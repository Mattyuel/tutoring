## Part B · Learn ⏱ ~8 min

### The place-value chart
| Hundred thousands | Ten thousands | Thousands | Hundreds | Tens | Ones |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | 4 | 7 | 3 | 0 | 6 |

**47 306** is read *forty-seven thousand, three hundred and six*.
- The **digit** 4 has a **value** of 40 000. The digit 7 has a value of 7 000.
- The **0** is a *placeholder* — it shows there are no tens. Without it, the number would be 4 736!
- We leave a small space (not a comma) between groups of three digits: 47 306.

**Each place is 10 times bigger than the place to its right.**
10 ones = 1 ten · 10 tens = 1 hundred · 10 hundreds = 1 thousand · 10 thousands = 1 ten thousand · 10 ten thousands = 1 hundred thousand

### Expanded form
47 306 = 40 000 + 7 000 + 300 + 6

### Renaming (the same number, said differently)
47 306 = 47 thousands and 306 ones = 473 hundreds and 6 ones = 4 730 tens and 6 ones

### Rounding
To round to the nearest **thousand**, look at the **hundreds** digit (the one to its right).
**0–4 → round down. 5–9 → round up.**
> 38 462 → hundreds digit is **4** → round down → **38 000**
> 38 562 → hundreds digit is **5** → round up → **39 000**

### Odd and even
A number is **even** if its ones digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Otherwise it is **odd**.
