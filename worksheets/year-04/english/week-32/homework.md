## 1 · Read

> ### How to Grow a Wattle from Seed
>
> Wattle seeds have a hard, waterproof coat. In the bush, the heat of a fire can crack it. At home, gardeners copy this by soaking the seeds in hot water, so that water can get inside and the seed can start to grow.
>
> **You will need:** 5–10 wattle seeds from a garden centre · a heatproof cup · hot water from a kettle (an adult must pour it) · a small pot with seed-raising mix · gardening gloves · a spray bottle of water
>
> 1. Put the seeds in the heatproof cup.
> 2. Ask an adult to pour hot water over the seeds until they are covered.
> 3. Leave the seeds to soak overnight.
> 4. The next day, choose the seeds that have swollen. These are ready to plant.
> 5. Wearing gloves, fill the pot with seed-raising mix and press it down lightly.
> 6. Plant each seed about 5 mm deep, then gently cover it.
> 7. Spray the soil until it is damp but not soggy. Keep it damp every day.
> 8. Place the pot in a warm, bright spot and wait patiently. Seedlings often appear within a few weeks.
>
> **Safety:** Always wear gloves when you handle potting or seed-raising mix, and wash your hands afterwards.
