## 3 · Word origins

| Word | Origin |
|---|---|
| *objective* | Latin *ob-* (in front of) + *iacere* (to throw): a thing "thrown before" the mind, existing outside us |
| *subjective* | Latin *sub-* (under) + *iacere*: belonging to the person, the "subject", who experiences it |
| *editor* | Latin *edere* (to put out, publish), from *e-* (out) + *dare* (to give) |

**7.** a) Use the origins to explain the difference between an *objective* report and a *subjective* one.
