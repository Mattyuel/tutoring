# Year 8 — WA Curriculum summary

> Working summary for planning. Verify against the SCSA K–10 Outline when needed.

## Mathematics

**Number & Algebra**
- Index notation with whole-number bases; index laws (multiply, divide, power of a power, zero index); square roots.
- Integers: all four operations; order of operations.
- Rational vs irrational numbers; terminating and recurring decimals; fractions ↔ decimals ↔ percentages.
- Percentages: increase/decrease, profit and loss, GST, discounts.
- Ratios and rates, including best buys and speed.
- Algebra: extend and apply the distributive law; factorise by common factors; simplify expressions.
- Linear equations (including with pronumerals on both sides); plot linear relations on the Cartesian plane.

**Measurement & Geometry**
- Units of measurement: area and volume conversions.
- Perimeter and area of parallelograms, trapeziums, rhombuses, kites.
- Circumference and area of circles; volume of prisms (incl. cylinders).
- Time: 24-hour time, time zones.
- Congruence of triangles (conditions); properties of quadrilaterals.

**Statistics & Probability**
- Events, complementary events, "and"/"or"; Venn diagrams and two-way tables.
- Sampling and populations; mean, median, mode, range; effect of outliers.

**Stretch (Year 9 content):** Pythagoras' theorem, scientific notation, expanding binomials, gradient and midpoint, simple interest, trigonometry introduction.

## English
- **Language:** how language varies by context; complex sentences with embedded and subordinate clauses; nominalisation; cohesion (reference, connectives); punctuation for effect; etymology (Latin/Greek roots).
- **Literature:** perspectives and representations in texts; how authors create voice; poetry and figurative language; comparing texts.
- **Literacy:** analyse persuasive and informative texts; evaluate sources; summarise; create analytical, persuasive and imaginative texts; formal spoken presentations.

## Science (content source for English texts)
- **Biological:** cells are the basic unit of living things; specialised cells; multicellular organisms have systems of organs.
- **Chemical:** particle model explains properties of solids, liquids and gases; elements, compounds and mixtures; chemical vs physical change.
- **Earth & Space:** sedimentary, igneous and metamorphic rocks; the rock cycle over geological time.
- **Physical:** energy appears in different forms (kinetic, potential, heat, light) and transfers/transforms within systems.
- **Science as a Human Endeavour:** scientific knowledge changes as new evidence arises; people in many cultures contribute.

## HASS (content source for English texts)
- **Civics & Citizenship:** freedoms in Australian democracy (speech, association, religion, movement); how laws are made (parliament and courts); types of law (statute, common, customary); citizenship, diversity and identity.
- **Economics & Business:** markets; how consumers and producers interact; influences on spending; work and income.
- **Geography:** landforms and landscapes (geomorphic processes, hazards); changing nations (urbanisation, migration).
- **History:** the ancient-to-modern transition — Medieval Europe (feudalism, the Church, the Black Death); the Ottoman Empire; Renaissance Italy; Polynesian expansion; Japan under the shoguns; the Mongol expansion; Angkor/Khmer Empire (WA selects specific depth studies).
