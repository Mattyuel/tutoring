# Year 4 — WA Curriculum summary

> Working summary for planning. Verify against the SCSA K–10 Outline when needed.

## Mathematics

**Number & Algebra**
- Place value: whole numbers to tens of thousands and beyond; partition, rearrange, regroup.
- Odd/even properties under addition and multiplication.
- Recall multiplication facts to 10 × 10 and related division facts.
- Mental and written strategies for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division (including remainders).
- Fractions: equivalent fractions; count by quarters, halves, thirds (incl. mixed numbers); place on a number line.
- Decimals: tenths and hundredths; link to fractions; money calculations and change.
- Number patterns from multiplication/addition; find unknown quantities in number sentences.

**Measurement & Geometry**
- Metric units for length, mass, capacity; scaled instruments.
- Time: am/pm, convert units of time, durations, timetables.
- Area by counting squares; compare angles to a right angle.
- Symmetry; combining shapes; maps with simple grid references and directions.

**Statistics & Probability**
- Chance: list outcomes, identify dependent/independent events (informal).
- Data: collect, organise (tables, column graphs), interpret; evaluate displays.

**Stretch (Year 5 content):** factors & multiples, multiply by 2-digit numbers, decimals to thousandths, compare fractions with related denominators, perimeter/area of rectangles, angle measurement in degrees.

## English
- **Language:** text structure & paragraphs; noun groups, verb groups, adverbials; clauses (simple, compound, complex intro); quoted/reported speech; apostrophes; subject-specific vocabulary.
- **Literature:** characters, settings, events; author's language choices (figurative language, imagery); create imaginative texts.
- **Literacy:** reading for main idea and detail; inference; text features (headings, diagrams, glossary); write informative, persuasive and narrative texts; plan, draft, edit.

## Science (content source for English texts)
- **Biological:** living things depend on each other and the environment; life cycles.
- **Chemical:** natural and processed materials have different properties suited to uses.
- **Earth & Space:** Earth's surface changes over time — natural processes (weathering, erosion) and human activity.
- **Physical:** forces can be exerted by one object on another (contact and non-contact — magnets, gravity, friction).
- **Science as a Human Endeavour:** science knowledge helps people understand the effects of their actions.

## HASS (content source for English texts)
- **Civics & Citizenship:** role of local government; rules vs laws and why they matter; how people participate in community; diverse groups in the community.
- **Economics & Business:** resources (natural, human, capital); scarcity; choices about wants and needs.
- **Geography:** continents — Africa and South America; environments and their importance; sustainable use and management of resources; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander custodial responsibility for Country.
- **History:** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander life before colonisation; European exploration and the first contacts (e.g. Dutch on the WA coast, 1600s); impact of exploration and settlement on First Nations peoples.
