# Western Australian Curriculum — overview

WA schools follow the **Western Australian Curriculum**, published by the
**School Curriculum and Standards Authority (SCSA)**. It is adapted from the Australian
Curriculum but differs in places (content sequencing, some year-level placements).

- Official source: SCSA K–10 Outline — <https://k10outline.scsa.wa.edu.au/>
- Each learning area has a **syllabus** per year level made of *content descriptions*
  and an *achievement standard*.
- The files in this folder are **working summaries** for planning. When precision
  matters (e.g. reporting to a parent), check the official syllabus.

## Learning areas used in this hub

| Learning area | Strands | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Number & Algebra · Measurement & Geometry · Statistics & Probability (+ proficiencies: understanding, fluency, problem-solving, reasoning) | Maths worksheets |
| English | Language · Literature · Literacy | English worksheets (skills) |
| Science | Science Understanding (Biological, Chemical, Earth & Space, Physical) · Science as a Human Endeavour · Science Inquiry Skills | Content for English texts |
| HASS | Civics & Citizenship · Economics & Business · Geography · History | Content for English texts |

## Pitching for advanced students

- Use the year-level content as the **floor**. Challenge sections may borrow from
  the next year level (noted in each `year-XX.md` under *Stretch*).
- Prefer depth (multi-step, reasoning, explaining) over racing ahead in content.
